# Privacy Policy: Phone Monitor

Effective date: 20 November 2026

Version française: PRIVACY_POLICY.fr.md

This policy explains what the Android app Phone Monitor: Device Health (package com.yan.phonemonitor, "the app") does with information on your phone. The app is published by mezyan ("we").

## In short

• Everything the app records stays on your phone.
• The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
• There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking libraries.
• We never see, collect, sell or share your data.
• Android cloud backup and device to device transfer are switched off for the app, so its data is not copied to Google Drive or to another phone.

## What the app reads, and why

The app is a device health tool. It reads information about your phone from Android and keeps the results in a private database on the phone.

• Battery level, charging state, temperature, voltage and current: for battery history and health, warnings, charging speed and the "Will it last?" planner. No permission is needed for this.
• Free and used storage, and the size of each app: for storage history, "What grew" and "Free up space". This uses usage access.
• Which apps were open and for how long, screen on and off, unlocks: for screen time, battery use per app, daily limits, recaps and apps that suddenly use more. This uses usage access, which you switch on yourself in Android settings.
• WiFi and mobile data used by each app: for data usage, data limits and the mobile data plan. This uses usage access, and on Android 9 and older the phone state permission, which Android requires there for data statistics. The app does not read your phone number, your calls or any device identifier.
• The list of installed apps, with their names, icons and the permissions they hold: for the app list, new and forgotten apps, app permissions and privacy watch. This uses the permission to see all installed apps.

The app does not use an accessibility service or notification access. It cannot read your screen, your messages or your notifications.

The app also uses these permissions, which give it no access to personal data: notifications (warnings, limits and recaps), foreground service (the live RAM test and fast readings, always shown with a notification while they run), wake lock and start at boot (readings in the background), vibration, and network state (to tell whether data went over WiFi or mobile).

## Where the data is stored

All readings, settings and results are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else.

## Sharing

We do not sell or share data. The only way data leaves the app is when you choose Export in Settings, Data & backup: the app creates a file and opens Android's share menu, and you decide where it goes. From then on the file is handled by the app or service you chose, under its own privacy policy.

## How long data is kept, and how to delete it

• You choose how long history is kept in Settings, Data & backup, Keep history for: 7, 30, 90, 180 or 365 days, or forever. Older readings are deleted automatically once a day.
• Delete all history, in Settings, Data & backup, removes every recorded reading.
• Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything.

## Security

Your history never leaves the phone and is kept in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. If you export a file, keep it somewhere you trust: it contains your recorded history.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date. If a future version of the app ever sends data off the phone, that will be stated here and in the Google Play listing before that version is released, and it will be optional.
